# InfiniAIBook advertisement: three storylines

The one thing: **your own sources go in; cited, finished work comes out, on your machine.**
The one viewer: a person with a pile of documents and a question. The spine is one
real notebook (the community-garden sample) and the real numbers in it ("24 raised beds").

## A. "Click the citation" (recommended: it starts on the product's proof, then widens)

- **World / device / form:** the real workspace on flat colour fields; the result first
  (a cited answer), one cursor that clicks its way through every feature; each click opens
  the next world (iris, whip, flood).
- **First two seconds:** a real cited answer, a cursor clicks citation 1, the source passage lights up.
- **Beats:** hook (cited answer) → tension (every file type piling up) → reveal (InfiniAIBook
  workspace) → proof (notes, search, talk, screen helper) → payoff (14 Studio formats, 33
  infographic styles, then the three real sample videos playing with their own sound) →
  claim (sources in, cited work out) → end card (mark, repo, command).
- **Look:** flat lamplight indigo, cool white and sun-yellow fields; the dark product UI is
  the only dark thing; Inter 900.
- **Sound:** no music; the three real sample videos keep their narration.
